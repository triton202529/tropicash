# Tropicash Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-06-30  
**Program:** Private Alpha

---

## 1. Introduction

Tropicash (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your choices during Private Alpha.

---

## 2. Data we collect

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Account** | Email, profile name, user ID |
| **Identity (KYC)** | Legal name, DOB, address, document images, selfie |
| **Financial** | Transaction history, wallet balance, funding/withdrawal records |
| **Technical** | IP address, device/browser data, session logs |
| **Support** | Messages and attachments you send us |
| **Compliance** | Screening results, fraud signals, case notes (admin-only) |

---

## 3. How we use data

- Provide wallet, funding, transfer, and withdrawal services  
- Verify identity and comply with AML/KYC obligations  
- Detect fraud and protect account security  
- Operate admin review, audit, and incident response  
- Improve service reliability and support users  

---

## 4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on jurisdiction: contract performance, legal obligation (AML/KYC), legitimate interests (fraud prevention), and consent where required.

---

## 5. Sharing

We may share data with:

- **Payment providers** (e.g., PayPal) to process funding and payouts  
- **Infrastructure providers** (hosting, database) under contract  
- **Authorities** when required by law or to report suspicious activity  
- **Professional advisors** under confidentiality  

We do **not** sell personal data.

---

## 6. Storage & security

- Data stored in encrypted infrastructure (Supabase and related services)  
- KYC documents in private buckets with signed URL access  
- Role-based admin access with audit logging  
- Retention per AML Policy (minimum 5 years where required)  

---

## 7. Your rights

Depending on jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, or portability. Contact support. Deletion may be limited by legal retention requirements.

---

## 8. Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. Optional analytics, if enabled, are disclosed in-app. See Cookie Policy section in Compliance Operations Manual.

---

## 9. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate, with appropriate safeguards.

---

## 10. Children

Tropicash is not directed to persons under 18.

---

## 11. Changes

We will post updated versions with effective dates. Material changes may require renewed acknowledgment.

---

## 12. Contact

Privacy inquiries: support channels listed in-app.
